FAQ

Questions founders ask when the cash gets loud.

What is burn rate?

Burn rate is the amount of cash a business uses over a period of time. This calculator focuses on net monthly burn: monthly expenses minus monthly revenue.

What is startup runway?

Runway is the estimated amount of time your current cash can support operations at your current net burn rate.

When should I start looking for funding?

Start before the emergency. If runway is under six months, begin reviewing funding options, documents, revenue trends, and repayment capacity now.

Does this guarantee funding eligibility?

No. This tool provides estimates only. Funding eligibility, terms, speed, and approval depend on underwriting and provider requirements.
